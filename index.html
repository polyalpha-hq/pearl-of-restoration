INDUSTRIAL OIL REGENERATION SYSTEM

Thermogravitational separation removes water and particulates without stripping additives. Adsorption columns restore the oxidation index. An additive dosing module brings oil back to full specification. No consumables except sorbent. Single operator, 24/7 continuous operation.

Capacity: 4,000,000 L/yr  ·  Cost: <$0.01/L without sorbent  ·  Payback: <12 months
BIO-BASED METALWORKING FLUID CONCENTRATE

Produced from regenerated industrial oil. Concentrate ratio 1:20–1:40. Compatible with ferrous and non-ferrous metals. Bio-stable formula, low foam, no biocides required.

Currently supplied to heavy manufacturing facilities
ACTIVATED CARBON FROM ORGANIC WASTE

Raw material: depleted molasses — an industrial byproduct that sugar factories pay to discard. Output: BAU/DAU grade activated carbon sorbent. Capacity: 134,000 t/year per complex. Payback: 6–8 months.

Applications: water treatment · gas purification · pharmaceutical · food industry
WASTE-TO-ENERGY COMPLEX

Processes MSW, plastics, tyres, oil sludge and agricultural waste. Output: liquid fuel, syngas and electricity. Modular design: 1–20+ t/hr. Zero toxic residue.

IRR: 18–45%  ·  Payback: 1–4 years
